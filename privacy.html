Daily Hub – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy describes how the Daily Hub Android app (package com.hidzhov.dailyhub, "the app") handles information. The app is developed by Ivan Hidzhov ("I", "me").

Information I collect

The app does not require an account and I do not collect, store or sell any personal information on my own servers. I do not have a backend.

Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, which may process data according to their own policies:

  • Google AdMob – shows advertisements. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and similar data to serve and measure ads and, where permitted, personalize them. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.
  • NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day API (api.nasa.gov) – the app requests the daily picture. Your IP address is visible to this service as with any internet request.
  • Useless Facts API (uselessfacts.jsph.pl) – the app requests random facts. Your IP address is visible to this service as with any internet request.

Consent and ad personalization

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where required are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time via the form in the app or in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization).

Data stored on your device

The app stores a temporary cache of the latest NASA picture data on your device so it loads faster. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Sharing features

When you use "Share" or "Share as Image", the content is handed to the app you choose through Android's share sheet. I do not receive it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this address, with the date above.
